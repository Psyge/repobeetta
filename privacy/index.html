Privacy Policy for RepoTracker.fi

General RepoTracker.fi is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal information such as names or email addresses directly. However, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous data, such as:

  • Browser type and device info.
  • Time spent on the site and pages visited.
  • Approximate location (country/city). This data is used solely to improve the user experience and monitor the technical performance of the service.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
